Open Source Code of Conduct
1. Purpose
The goal of this Code of Conduct is to foster a welcoming, safe, and collaborative environment for everyone participating in this open source project and its community spaces.

We are committed to making participation a harassment‑free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio‑economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

2. Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to:

Project spaces: issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, documentation, and repositories.

Community spaces: chat servers, forums, social media, and any other project‑related communication channels.

In‑person or virtual events: meetups, calls, conferences, or hackathons organized or endorsed by the project.

It applies to all participants, including maintainers, contributors, users, and anyone representing the project.

3. Our standards
3.1 Expected behavior
Respect others: Use welcoming and inclusive language; be considerate of differing viewpoints and experiences.

Be constructive: Offer helpful feedback, focus on solutions, and assume good faith where possible.

Collaborate openly: Share knowledge, document decisions, and help others learn and grow.

Respect boundaries: Honor people’s time, attention, and personal limits; accept “no” as an answer.

Protect privacy: Do not share private or identifying information about others without explicit permission.

Follow project guidelines: Adhere to contribution guidelines, coding standards, and review processes.

3.2 Unacceptable behavior
Harassment and abuse: Offensive comments, personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.

Discrimination: Any demeaning or derogatory comments related to identity or personal characteristics.

Sexualized content or behavior: Unwelcome sexual attention, imagery, or advances of any kind.

Threats or intimidation: Threatening violence, encouraging self‑harm, or inciting others to harass.

Doxxing or privacy violations: Publishing others’ private information (e.g., address, email, real name) without consent.

Malicious interference: Intentionally introducing harmful code, sabotaging work, or abusing project infrastructure.

4. Responsibilities of maintainers
Clarify standards: Maintain clear contribution guidelines and enforce this Code consistently.

Take action: Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code.

Moderate spaces: Temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Lead by example: Model respectful, inclusive behavior in all project interactions.

5.1 Reporting an incident

What happened (description and any links/screenshots)

Where and when it occurred

Who was involved or present

Any other information you believe is relevant

Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent possible and reviewed by the maintainers or a designated response team.

5.2 Enforcement process
Initial review: The maintainers will review the report, may request additional information, and assess the severity.

Possible actions include:

Informal conversation or clarification of expectations

Warning to the reported individual

Temporary or permanent removal from project spaces

Reversal of contributions (e.g., reverting commits, closing issues)

Escalation to hosting platforms or event organizers, if applicable

Enforcement decisions are made at the discretion of the maintainers and are considered final.
